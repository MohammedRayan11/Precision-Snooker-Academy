Our Story

Five Years in the Making —
A Vision of Precision

Precision Snooker Academy was born from the passion of Mohammed Faisal Khan — a man who spent five dedicated years building what Bangalore truly deserved: one of the most premium snooker academies in the city. His mission was singular — to create an international atmosphere with world-class equipment, and deliver an experience worthy of the game's greatest stages.

From championship-grade tables to IBSF and WST certified Aramith balls, every element at PSA is chosen to serve the player. Whether you're a seasoned competitor or discovering the sport for the first time, this is where snooker is elevated to an art form.
