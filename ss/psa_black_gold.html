Our Story

A Space Built
With Love & Passion

Precision Snooker Academy is Bangalore's most premium destination for cue sports enthusiasts. Nestled in the heart of Kammanahalli, we've created a sanctuary where the art of snooker is celebrated with the reverence it deserves.

Whether you're a seasoned player refining your technique or a curious newcomer discovering the game for the first time, PSA offers an unmatched environment — world-class tables, expert guidance, and an atmosphere that breathes snooker.
